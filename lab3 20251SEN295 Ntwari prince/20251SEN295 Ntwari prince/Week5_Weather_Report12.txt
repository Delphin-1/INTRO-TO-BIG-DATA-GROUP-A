This lab analyzed 60 daily weather records from Kigali using **NumPy and Boolean masks.

 3. Temperature Trend

The first 30 days had an average temperature of 26.21°C, while the last 30 days averaged 21.78°C.

This represents a decrease of approximately 4.43°C, showing that the recorded period became cooler over time.

 4. Conclusion

The analysis shows that NumPy and Boolean masks can efficiently calculate weather statistics and identify specific conditions without using loops. The temperature decreased noticeably during these 60 records, although this result applies only to this dataset and does not represent Kigali's long-term climate.
